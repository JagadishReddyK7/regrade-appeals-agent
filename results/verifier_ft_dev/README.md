# Fine-tuned verifier: training, verifier comparison and pipeline run on dev (2026-11-12)

One Kaggle session (`notebooks/verifier_ft.ipynb`, T4 x2), commit `620d60e` (`commit.txt`,
branch `feat/verifier-training`), transformers 5.19.0, peft 0.21.2, bitsandbytes 0.50.2,
vLLM 0.32.0 (`pip_freeze.txt`), temperature 0, seed 0. The pipeline code and prompts are
the frozen version (`docs/DECISIONS.md` section 13); only the verifier changes. Every
`metrics.json` was recomputed locally from `predictions.jsonl` and matches (the pipeline
run's `metrics_rescored.json`).

Not kept here: the adapter and the merged model (weights stay in the Kaggle notebook
output), the training set and the held-out set (regenerated by
`python -m verifier.train_data` from the committed situation files, seed 0).

## Training (`verifier_train-20261112-185927-2feaa3/`)

Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, QLoRA (4-bit NF4, float16 compute, r 16, attention projections),
594 examples, none too long, 300 steps of 2 (about one epoch), learning rate 1e-4, 2,800 s,
peak 12.1 GB. Mean loss per 50 steps: 0.165, 0.063, 0.049, 0.026, 0.032, 0.009. The
generation check after training: 6 of 6 answers valid and correct.

## Verifier comparison on the same claims (`verifier_eval-*`)

Dev claims: each claim the pipeline's verifier judged in dev runs 2 and 3, with exactly
the evidence it saw (`eval_dev_v2.jsonl`, `eval_dev_v3.jsonl`, `verifier/eval_data.py`),
gold verdict from the case. Dev is a held-out assignment (D1): the verifier was never
trained on it.

| Verifier | dev v3 (30) acc. | macro-F1 | tone sens. | dev v2 (23) acc. | macro-F1 | tone sens. | invalid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Untuned 3B (A4a) | 0.27 | 0.26 | 0.88 | 0.39 | 0.52 | 0.83 | 0 |
| Prompted 7B (A4b, frozen pipeline) | 0.60 | 0.41 | 0.50 | 0.48 | 0.45 | 0.33 | 0 |
| **Fine-tuned 3B** | **0.73** | **0.70** | 0.63 | **0.70** | **0.69** | 0.50 | 0 |

Tone sensitivity: share of claim groups (same situation, turn and item) whose verdict
differs across tones; only 8 (v3) and 6 (v2) groups, so it is noisy.

- The untuned 3B sends most claims to a human (19 of 30 needs_human on v3, 1 correctly).
- The prompted 7B is too strict: it refutes 11 of 21 supported claims (v3) and never
  says needs_human.
- The fine-tuned 3B's main error is the same direction but smaller: 6 of 21 supported
  claims refuted (v3). It wrongly supports 1 refuted claim per set.

**Held-out tone set** (`*_heldout`, 184 examples: 46 pairs x 4 tones, authority never
trained): fine-tuned 3B 0.96 accuracy, macro-F1 0.97, tone sensitivity 0.11 (untuned 3B
0.40 / 0.40 / 0.33; prompted 7B 0.63 / 0.41 / 0.26). **This is in-distribution**: same
assignments, submissions and situations as training, only new wordings and pairings.
It shows the authority tone does not flip the fine-tuned verifier (authority 0.96, as the
trained tones), not that the verifier generalises; the dev numbers above are the ones
to report as quality.

## Pipeline with the fine-tuned verifier (`pipeline_ft_dev-20261112-203726-2e8b75/`)

| Pipeline, fine-tuned 3B verifier | 0.67 | 0.00 | 0.43 | 0.80 | 1.00 | 0.05 | 0.67 | 0.00 | 0.50 | 1 | 19.5 | 14,544 |

Case by case (first turn) against run 3: 16 vs 14 exact decisions.
- Gained: dev-002 and dev-010 (recursion allowed by the 12 September announcement, now
  supported in the first turn), dev-022 (arithmetic error in the total).
- Lost: dev-007. Its two claims are a faulty test (true) and a style complaint (false);
  the fine-tuned verifier got both wrong (refuted the test claim, supported style), so
  the pipeline gave 2 instead of 5 marks. In dev-005 (same situation) it made the same two
  errors; the outcome type became partial_change, but with 1 instead of 5 marks. This is
  why missed correction rose to 0.50, and it is a grade change for a false claim that the
  wrongful-change metric does not count (the total change stays below the gold change).
- dev-009: the verifier said needs_human, so the first turn escalated (the over-escalation
  0.05); the follow-up turn then gave the right full change.
- The faulty-test claim (situation D1-sit01) is refuted in all four tones (the prompted
  7B supported it once, in dev-007): the evidence agent runs the test but does not
  retrieve the specification, so neither verifier can compare them.

No invalid verifier output in the pipeline; dev-021 again failed in the extractor (span
not copied exactly, no decision), as in run 3. The 7B's vLLM log has 10 hermes
tool-parser errors (9 in run 3).

## Go/no-go (`docs/DECISIONS.md` section 14)

Both conditions hold on dev: macro-F1 0.70 vs 0.41 (v3) and 0.69 vs 0.45 (v2), and exact
accuracy 0.67 vs 0.58. **The fine-tuned 3B becomes the main verifier for the test runs**;
the prompted 7B is reported as ablation A4b and the untuned 3B as A4a. Caveats: 24 cases,
a difference of 2 cases, prompts designed on this set, one assignment; no comparison here
is statistically significant.
